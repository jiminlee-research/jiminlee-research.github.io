Research Interests

Mental Health & Health Disparities

Understanding inequities in psychosocial distress, related health outcomes, and suicidality experienced by minoritized populations.

Stigma & Discrimination

Examining how personal and structural stigma shape mental health and behavioral health outcomes.

Behavioral Health Services

Improving the accessibility and responsiveness of behavioral health services for people affected by trauma and marginalization through a multilevel lens.

Community-Engaged Research

Connecting research with practice through collaboration with community organizations.

Health Equity & Policy

Advancing rights-based, evidence-informed policies for more equitable health and social service systems.
